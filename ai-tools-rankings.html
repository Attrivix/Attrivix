How we score (100 points)

What counts as an AI tool: the AI makes or changes a decision or action, such as scoring a loan, flagging fraud, reading documents, talking to members, or choosing who gets an offer. Tools whose AI only helps people view data (predictive dashboards, forecasts, chat-with-your-data) aren't ranked here. Data layers that feed AI aren't AI tools and aren't ranked.

Who scores: Attrivix scores every tool with the same rubric below.

  • Impact on funded loans and workflow (25): Does the tool change approvals, automation, funding speed or loss, measured on funded loans and not only apps?
  • CU fit (15): A CU-specific product, named CU customers, and stated core/LOS integrations.
  • Explainability and fair-lending compliance (15): Reason codes, fair-lending testing, and explainable outputs the vendor states publicly.
  • Data security (15): SOC 2 / ISO 27001 or similar stated on the vendor's own site. SOC 2 stated on the vendor's own site scores 13. SOC 2 plus the vendor's own ISO 27001 certification scores 14. SOC 2 reported only by third parties scores 10. If nothing was stated, the tool gets 4 to 6 points, which is not a judgment that it's insecure.
  • Time to value and IT burden (10): Implementation time and the integration lift the vendor states.
  • Pricing transparency (10): Public pricing or a clear pricing model. "Not disclosed" scores 2.
  • Evidence (10): Named CU case studies with measurable results and a source.

We score only from public sources. Unknown facts show as "not disclosed." Vendors can request a correction.

What "vendor-reported" means: Outcome numbers on the cards (for example, a drop in delinquency or more deals funded per day) come from each vendor's own published case study or press release. We link the source, but these figures have not been independently verified. Our score is our assessment; the outcome figure is the vendor's claim.
