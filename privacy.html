1. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • The website at attrivix.com and the pages we operate there, including the homepage, The CU Shortlist, the AI Tools Rankings page, the one-pager, this privacy policy, and the support page.
  • Information you send us by email, through The CU Shortlist signup forms, or through a Microsoft Bookings scheduling link.
  • How Attrivix treats credit union member data when we provide the Attrivix product, as described in section 2. The credit union’s own privacy policy and our agreement with that credit union also apply to that data, and they control if there is a conflict about member data.

This website is intended for U.S. visitors, primarily credit union professionals.

2. The Attrivix product and credit union member data

Attrivix is the enterprise data layer for credit unions. It unifies data from systems that do not talk to each other — such as the core, loan origination system, call center, CRM, marketing tools, and website analytics — inside the credit union’s own Microsoft tenant, so the credit union can have a connected, 360-degree member view. Marketing attribution is one use of that layer. Attrivix is not an AI tool. It can feed AI tools a credit union already uses, but AI is not required to use Attrivix.

When we provide the product to a credit union:

  • The product runs in the credit union’s own Microsoft tenant.
  • The credit union is the controller of its member data. Member data comes from the credit union’s own source of truth, connected in that tenant. Outside vendor data can be added on top at the credit union’s direction.
  • Attrivix processes that member data only as a service provider, on the credit union’s instructions, and under our agreement with the credit union.
  • We do not use credit union member nonpublic personal information from the product for our own marketing, and we do not sell it.

If you are a credit union member and have a question about your personal information held by your credit union, please contact your credit union. Attrivix does not decide how that institution uses its member data.

9. Credit unions and nonpublic personal information

Credit unions are financial institutions. Their member information may be nonpublic personal information protected by the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and other banking privacy rules, and by the credit union’s own privacy notice.

When Attrivix provides the product to a credit union, we handle that member information as a service provider to the credit union, not as the financial institution that collected it from the member. Member data used in the product is processed in the credit union’s Microsoft tenant, on the credit union’s instructions, and for the purposes set out in our agreement with that credit union. We do not disclose that member nonpublic personal information to third parties for our own independent marketing.

This website policy does not replace the credit union’s privacy notice to its members, and it does not give Attrivix the right to use member data in ways the agreement does not allow.
