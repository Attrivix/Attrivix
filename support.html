How to get in touch

Attrivix is the enterprise data layer for credit unions. It unifies data from systems that do not talk to each other inside the credit union’s own Microsoft tenant. If you have a question about the product, this website, the AI Tools Rankings, or The CU Shortlist, here is how to reach us.

What to expect

We read every message and will get back to you as soon as we can. If something is urgent, start a chat with us using the chat bubble at the bottom right of this page. The best ways to reach us otherwise are email or a booked call.

What to include in a request

A short, specific note helps us get back to you faster. Please include:

  • Your name and the name of your credit union or organization, if applicable.
  • The best email address for a reply.
  • Whether your question is about the Attrivix product, a Microsoft Marketplace listing, this website, or The CU Shortlist.
  • A brief description of what you need, including any relevant dates or error messages.
  • For product questions, it helps to know which systems are involved (for example, core, LOS, CRM, call center, or marketing tools). To protect your members, please leave account numbers and other nonpublic personal information out of your first email. If we need that information, we will set up a secure way to receive it.

Product and member data

The Attrivix product runs in the credit union’s own Microsoft tenant. The credit union is the controller of its member data. Attrivix processes that data only on the credit union’s instructions under its agreement. If you are a credit union member and have a question about your own information, your credit union is the best place to start.

Attrivix is not an AI tool. It can feed AI tools a credit union already uses, but you do not need AI to use Attrivix.

Privacy

Our privacy policy explains what information we collect on this website, how credit union member data is treated in the product, and how to make a privacy request. Send privacy requests or unsubscribe questions to patricia@attrivix.com. A subject line like “California privacy request” or “Unsubscribe” is helpful, but not required.
