Identity resolution: deterministic matching on normalized email/phone/name with corroboration across fields, plus a funding-date-window tier for otherwise-ambiguous name collisions (515 of 1,767 members in this file share a full name with someone else). A blind edit-distance fuzzy-email fallback was tested and rejected — it raised recall 1.4pp at a 3pp precision cost, not a good trade for attribution accuracy. Attribution: standard U-shape (40% first touch / 40% last touch / 20% split across assisted touches), computed only over touches this pipeline could confidently resolve — "Loans fully reconciled" above flags when recovered touches don't fully reconcile to 100% of the funded amount, rather than silently rounding them off.
