Form 1098-VLI: When the Form Is Ready but the Fields Aren't

Form 1098-VLI is showing up in year-end work for a lot of credit unions. The form is ready. The fields it needs to work are often somewhere else entirely.

Patricia Thomas 2 min read Read full post ⌄

For credit unions with auto lending, direct and indirect. Drawn from T.D. 10054 (Sept. 8, 2026) and the IRS draft Instructions for Form 1098-VLI (Rev. December 2026). Not tax advice.

Form 1098-VLI is showing up in year-end work for a lot of credit unions. Cores are building it into servicing. Members will get statements. That side of it is underway.

Box 1 is qualifying interest. To get there you need deal pieces like negative equity, cash-out, and related add-ons, tied to the interest the core received on that loan. Those pieces often sit on the deal or in the LOS. They are not always clean fields on the servicing system.

Same rules whether the loan came in direct or indirect. The difference is where the fields live.

CU*Answers has said production is a servicing job, and that negative equity and refinance math is the credit union's to track:

"The calculations considering negative equity amounts, refinances, etc., that will need to be tracked by the credit union... We will report interest paid on the loan, but we are not providing any calculations." (CU*Answers Kitchen)

What has to meet in one place

At year-end you need a clean join across deal structure (negative equity, cash-out, what was financed), vehicle and eligibility facts, interest received on that loan, and enough to run the per-loan $600 test on qualifying interest.

If any of that is missing, or it only exists as a funded total, Box 1 is a guess.
